What WAPE measures

The headline metric of retail forecasting, and the three metrics that keep it honest.

WAPE (weighted absolute percentage error) is total absolute error divided by total actual sales, over every item and week you score:

WAPE = Σ |actual − forecast| / Σ actual
  • Read it as units got wrong per unit sold. WAPE 0.40 means 40 mis-forecast units for every 100 sold. Retail teams often quote 1 − WAPE as forecast accuracy.
  • It works with zeros. MAPE divides by each week’s actual and blows up on a zero-sales week, which is most weeks for a slow seller.
  • It is volume-weighted. A hit selling 200 a week moves WAPE far more than a slow item selling 2, so WAPE tracks the business impact of errors, not the average series.
  • It rewards the median. Absolute error is minimised by the median, so for a sparse item a forecast of 0 can score well while leaving the shelf empty. Always report bias next to WAPE.
